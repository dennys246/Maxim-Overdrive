| D31 | **Load is part of the world's physics (owner, 2026-11-22).** D22 already plans collapse by a min cut of the intact cross-section in metres (§3). This ruling adds load against capacity: crushing, bending and buckling, coupled to fire through strength by mass fraction and temperature (`world_v2.md` §3.2), so a table whose legs char through under its load falls. Capacity is judged on geometry in metres, and a cell-quantized remaining section is a known risk to the outcome class, because capacity scales with area, b·h² or b⁴. Each law ships with a closed-form test (a column's crushing load, Euler buckling, a simply supported beam's bending stress, a statically indeterminate four-legged table's load split, and a member weakened by fire) and a cross-grid test. Structural members thinner than the thin-feature rule allows as cells (§1.1), such as a table's legs, need a contract §1.1 doesn't have yet: its fuel objects are fuel, never structure. Designing that contract is part of the research, in the Structure and motion entry of `plans/long_term_features.md`. | owner, 2026-11-22 | cites §1.1, §3; `world_v2.md` §3.2, §4 | live |
