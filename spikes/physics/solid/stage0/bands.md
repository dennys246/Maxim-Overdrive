## Interpretations (choices the pre-registration leaves open)

- m''_10: raw[i] = (m[i-2] - m[i+2]) / (t[i+2] - t[i-2]) / A (MaCFP-4's `Calculate_dm_dt`), then the mean of the finite raw values with |t_j - t_i| <= 5 s.
- m''_60: mass linearly interpolated at t - 30 and t + 30 s between finite samples; undefined within 30 s of either end of the record.
- PMMA mass lost: raw mass at the t_ig sample minus raw mass at the peak sample, over the area (g/m²).
- ΔH_c (PMMA, and MaCFP-2's outlier rule): trapezoidal ∫Q'' dt over [t_ig, last Q'' >= 24] over the raw mass lost between those samples. A test whose ΔH_c can't be computed (no mass signal) fails the rule.
- Pine window: from t_ig to the first sample after the first peak with m''_60 < 2.7 g/m²s; the plateau's [t_ig + 250, t_ig + 450 s] is cut at the window's end. With no such sample the window runs to the record's end.
- PMMA envelopes take every PMMA cone file at 25, 50 and 65 (DBI's round samples included, at their README area 0.00387 m²), minus MaCFP-2's outliers and §2.1's artefacts. Only t_ig's is graded (P1); the others are reported for §6 outcome 4.
- The artefact rule's lab median is over the lab's tests still in the set at that flux, the test itself included.
- Pine per-lab bands use the same tests as the envelopes. Pine ΔH_c,eff is the mean over the fit tests at 30 and 60 pooled (also given per flux).
- m''_ext: m''_60 at each README flame-out time, for the fit-flux tests of the labs that record one (Aalto 30 and 60, UQ 60); a flame-out within 30 s of the record's end has no m''_60 and is listed as such.
- P0: back T is the mean of the thermocouples reading at each sample; half the sample mass is half the mass file's first reading. Widening uses each feature's V tolerance (peak 2.5%, peak time 8 s, the other times 3 s); the half-mass time and back-T crossings have no V row, so they take §2.2's 3 s on times.
